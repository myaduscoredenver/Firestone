How these estimates were calculated

The local model uses 69 nearby two-story resales since 2022 and accounts for above-grade area, finished and unfinished lower-level area, lot size, age, garage, beds, baths, neighborhood and sale date. It assumes two garage spaces for the subject. The model has no verified condition or remodeling input. In a small test on five comparable 2026 Oak Meadows sales, the average absolute error was about $16,000. Actual errors varied. This is a pricing discussion tool.
